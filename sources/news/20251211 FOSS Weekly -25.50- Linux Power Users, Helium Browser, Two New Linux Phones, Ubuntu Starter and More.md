## 📰 Linux and Open Source News

  * Proton has come up with [a solid Google Sheets alternative][6].
  * Valve is showing everyone [a better way to fund open source][7].
  * A significant [drag-and-drop vulnerability][8] has been found in WebKitGTK.
  * Calibre eBook reader now [lets you chat about your eBooks using local AI][9].
  * Come Fedora 44, the trusty kernel console will be replaced with [a safer, modern alternative][10].
  * The [Agentic AI Foundation][11] is here to provide neutral governance for developing agentic AI technologies.

## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In Nemo file manager, go to `Edit → Preferences`. Here, in the " _Context Menu_ s" section, enable the _Duplicate_ , _Copy to_ , and _Move to_ options. Now you can right-click on a file and either duplicate it, copy it, or move it without needing to use keyboard shortcuts.
