This is an Open Source 3D Printer That You Can Build Yourself
======
Lemontron is an open source 3D printer that you can build yourself.
Sounds exciting!
[![][1]][2]

[3D printing][3] has come a long way in recent years, with many people and organizations investing a considerable amount of time and resources into perfecting their processes. If you're not familiar, 3D printing is a method of fabricating physical three-dimensional objects by taking in [CAD][4] models and material as input.

Many tinkerers go the extra mile by creating **open-source 3D printers** that fellow DIYers or newcomers can easily build when they have the right parts and tools.

One such innovator has recently introduced an exciting new 3D printer that **combines affordability with impressive functionality** , making it an accessible option for anyone looking to dive into the world of 3D printing.

### Lemontron: What To Expect?

![The Lemontron can print zesty lemons. 🍋][5]

A brainchild of [James Loper][6], the Lemontron is **a fused filament fabrication (**[ **FFF**][7] **) 3D printer** that takes inspiration and a key component ( _the Positron drive_ ) from the popular [Positron V3.2 kit][8].

As an upside-down printer, it can be used to create high-quality prints for custom parts, prototyping, hobby projects, art and much more.

The **key specs** of the Lemontron include:

  * A unibody chassis.
  * An internal extruder.
  * A 180 × 180 × 165 mm print volume.
  * The [Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W][9] as the brains.
  * Power duties being handled by a [24V power supply][10].
  * And the cooling being handled by 2x [2510 chassis fans][11] for general airflow, 1x [3010 fan][12] for cooling the tool head and heat break, and 1x [5015 fan][13] for pushing air through components like the hotend.



James [compares][14] the build plate on the Lemontron to the likes of the [Original Prusa Mini+][15] and [Bambulab A1 Mini][16], with **the overall design being quite compact** that provides superb portability.

He also adds that **the modest price for building a Lemontron is due to the absence of custom** [**CNC**][17] **parts** that usually bump up the cost of such printers by a great deal. This approach allows anyone to build a 3D printer using off-the-shelf parts.

### Build Lemontron

Watch the whole thing—top-tier commentary awaits! 🤌

To begin, you can refer to the [Bill of Materials][18] ( _BOM_ ) to get a list of the required parts for building the Lemontron. Expect the total cost to be around **$413** ( _the BOM mentions $411.58_ ) plus any shipping fees and taxes you might have to pay.

Thereafter, you can refer to the [official guide][19] _(currently in the making)_ and the YouTube video linked above to build the printer. If you run into any issues, then you can consider joining the [Voxolite][20] Discord server, where James is quite active under the Lemontron moniker.
