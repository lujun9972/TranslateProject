Match the Resolution: New Year Intentions Meet Linux Commands 🎯
======

Every year we make resolutions: clean up our lives, start fresh, commit to change, and stay organized. But did you know your Linux system has commands that perfectly mirror these intentions? In this fun matching puzzle, you'll connect common New Year resolutions with their Linux command equivalents.

Whether you are decluttering files like decluttering your life or rebooting your habits like rebooting your system, this quiz shows that maybe your terminal understands your aspirations better than you thought!

Let's see if you can match the resolution with the right command.
