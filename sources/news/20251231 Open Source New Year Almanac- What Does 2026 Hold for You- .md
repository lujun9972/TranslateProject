Open Source New Year Almanac: What Does 2026 Hold for You? 🗓️
======

We are not talking horoscopes here but more like your [almanac][1] reading. This personality quiz is more like holding up a mirror to your habits and seeing what reflects back.

Do you dig through old changelogs for fun? Can't resist experimenting with something half-broken? Always thinking about the community? Or are you the quiet one who automates everything in the background?

Answer 10 questions about how you react to projects, systems, and change. By the end, you'll discover whether you are an _**Archivist of Time**_ , a _**Tinkerer of Possibilities**_ , a _**Steward of the Commons**_ , or a _**Quiet System Shaper**_.
