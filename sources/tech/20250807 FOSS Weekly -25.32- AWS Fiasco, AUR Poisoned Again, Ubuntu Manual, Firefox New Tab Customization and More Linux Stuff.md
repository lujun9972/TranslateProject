## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In GNOME, you can arrange the order of appearance of search results. Open _Settings_ and go into the " _Search_ " section. Here, make sure that " _App Search_ " is enabled, and then reorder the items according to your needs.

![][37]

In the screenshot above, the _Files_ result made it to the top as I had changed its priority.
