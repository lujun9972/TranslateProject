## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In KDE Konsole, you can adjust the number of Scrollback lines dynamically. To achieve that, first right-click on an empty place and select “ _Adjust Scrollback…_ ”.

![][32]

Now, enter a value for the desired number of Scrollback lines by choosing the “ _Fixed size:_ ” option and click on “ _OK_ ”.

![][33]

Remember, this is temporary and only affects the current session. Furthermore, having a huge number of Scrollback lines might cause performance issues, since all of this is stored in the RAM.

## 🗓️ Tech Trivia

[Ada Lovelace][36], born on December 10, 1815, as the daughter of poet Lord Byron, was a 19th-century mathematician who worked with [Charles Babbage][37] on the [Analytical Engine][38], writing what is considered the first computer program. Honoring her legacy, the ADA programming language, based on Pascal, was developed in the 1970s for readability and maintainability.
