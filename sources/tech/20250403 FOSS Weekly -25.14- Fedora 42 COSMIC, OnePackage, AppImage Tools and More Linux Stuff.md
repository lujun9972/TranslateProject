## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In KDE Plasma, you can edit copied texts in the _Clipboard_. First, launch the clipboard using the shortcut _CTRL+V_. Now, click on the _Edit_ button, which looks like a pencil.

![][26]

Then, edit the contents and click on _Save_ to store it as a new clipboard item.

## 🗓️ Tech Trivia

On March 31, 1939, Harvard and IBM signed an agreement to build the [Mark I][29], also known as the IBM Automatic Sequence Controlled Calculator ( _ASCC_ ).

This pioneering electromechanical computer, conceived by [Howard Aiken][30], interpreted instructions from paper tape and data from punch cards, playing a significant role in World War II calculations.
