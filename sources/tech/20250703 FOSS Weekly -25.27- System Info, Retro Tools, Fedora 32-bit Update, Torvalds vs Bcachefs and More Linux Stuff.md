## 📰 Linux and Open Source News

  * [digiKam 8.7][5] is here with many upgrades.
  * [Tyr][6] is a new Rust-based driver for Arm Mali GPUs.
  * Claudia is [an open source GUI solution][7] for Claude AI coding.
  * Broadcom has been [bullying enterprises][8] with VMware audits.
  * Google has donated [the A2A protocol][9] to the Linux foundation.
  * [Murena Fairphone (Gen. 6)][10] has been introduced with some decent specs.
  * [Warp 2.0][11] is here with AI agents, better terminal tools, and more.
  * Cloudflare has released [Orange Me2eets][12], an E2EE video calling solution.
  * Bazzite was looking [at a grim future][13]. Luckily, the proposal to retire 32-bit support on Fedora [has been dropped][14], for now.

## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

The Dolphin file manager offers you a selection mode. To activate it, press the `Space bar`.

In this view, you can single click on a file/folder to select them. Here, you will notice that a quick access bar appears at the bottom when you select items, offering actions like Copy, Cut, Rename, Move to Trash, etc.

## 🗓️ Tech Trivia

The [IBM 650][37], introduced on July 2, 1953, was one of the first widely used computers, featuring a magnetic drum for storage and using punch cards for programming. With a memory capacity of 20,000 decimal digits, it became a workhorse for businesses and universities throughout the 1950s.
