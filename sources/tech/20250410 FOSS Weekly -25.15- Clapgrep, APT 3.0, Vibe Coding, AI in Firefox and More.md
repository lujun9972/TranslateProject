## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In Firefox, you can delete temporary browsing data using the " _Forget_ " button. First, right-click on the toolbar and select " _Customize Toolbar_ ".

![][27]

Now, from the list, drag and drop the " _Forget_ " button to the toolbar. If you click on it, you will be asked to clear 5 min, 2 hrs, and 24 hrs of browsing data, pick any one of them and click on "Forget!".

![][28]
