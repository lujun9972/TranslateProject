Murena Teams Up With German Manufacturer to Offer a €698 de-Googled Android Tablet
======

[![Warp Terminal][1]][2]

[Murena][3] has been in the business of selling de-Googled Android smartphones loaded with /e/OS since 2022. The French company strips out Google services and offers devices that don't profit from its users' data.

On the other side, [Volla][4] is a German hardware maker known for building repairable, privacy-focused devices. Founded in 2017 by [Dr. Jörg Wurzer][5], they sell smartphones and a tablet preloaded with [Volla OS][6] and [Ubuntu Touch][7].

Now these two have partnered up to offer the _**Murena Volla Tablet**_ , which combines Volla's hardware with Murena's software.

### Murena Volla Tablet: What's Inside?

![The front and rear views of the Murena Volla Tablet.][8]

The device is powered by a [MediaTek Helio G99][9] SoC with **12 GB of RAM**. This is not a flagship chip, but it should be able to handle everyday tasks comfortably.

Storage is generous at **512 GB** , and you can **expand it up to 1 TB** using a microSD card. That is plenty of room for apps, files, and media without worrying about running out of space for a long time ( _unless you are a hoarder_ ).

Don't worry, I won't make you scroll further. Here are the rest of the specs:

  * **GPU:** ARM Mali-G57 MC2
  * **Display:** 12.6″ 240 ppi QHD.
  * **Cameras:** 13MP rear camera, 5MP front camera.
  * **Battery:** 10,000 mAh, non-removable.
  * **Connectivity:** WiFi 6
  * **Cellular:** 2G, 3G, and 4G.



**For the software** , the Murena Volla Tablet comes equipped with the **Android 14-based version of /e/OS**. This strips out Google services and replaces them with open source alternatives.

You get tracker-free native apps like Mail, Calendar, Maps, and Camera out of the box. [Murena Workspace][10] is also included, offering cloud storage and office tools with 1 GB of free space.

If you are **wondering about Android apps** , the [App Lounge][11] lets you install them from [F-Droid][12], the [Play Store][13], and Murena's database of [PWAs][14]. Each app gets a privacy score from 0 to 10 based on its permissions and trackers, helping you decide what to install.

[Gaël Duval][15], the CEO of Murena, had a few words to say during the launch:

> Our users have long awaited a large-display tablet with /e/OS - one powerful enough to replace a laptop for everyday tasks, especially with keyboard and mouse support.
>
> Thanks to our partnership with Volla, that wait is over. We're excited to team up with an independent European company that shares our vision: technology should serve people, not the other way around.

### Get the Murena Volla Tablet

Priced at **€698** ( _excl. shipping_ ), the Murena Volla Tablet comes with **a 2-year warranty** that covers manufacturing defects. Things like scratches, degrading battery performance, and damage to the device after you throw it at your ex don't count.

You should also know that **the availability of this device is limited** to Europe, Hong Kong, Canada, Puerto Rico, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, South Korea, and the US.
