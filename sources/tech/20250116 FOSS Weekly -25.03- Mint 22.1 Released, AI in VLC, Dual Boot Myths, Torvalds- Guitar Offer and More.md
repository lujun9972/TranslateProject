## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

You can search for free icons from [Font Awesome][32] or [Nerd Fonts][33] to add to panels and terminal tools like Fastfetch.

Ensure that you [install the respective fonts][34], _font-awesome_ and _firacode-nerd_ on your system before using. Otherwise, they won't appear properly.

![][35]

On Font Awesome, click on _Copy Glyph_ to copy the icon to the clipboard. And in Nerd Fonts, click on the _Icons_ button to copy the icon to the clipboard.
