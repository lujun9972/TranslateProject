This Could Be The Linux Phone We All Have Been Waiting For
======

[![Warp Terminal][1]][2]

Emerging from the ashes of [Nokia][3] and [MeeGo][4] in 2011, [Jolla][5] is a Finnish company founded by former Nokia engineers. They continued developing Linux-powered mobile operating systems and devices when MeeGo was abandoned.

Their first product, the [Jolla Phone][6], launched in November 2013 after a successful crowdfunding campaign. It ran [Sailfish OS][7], a gesture-based Linux system that Jolla continues developing to this day.

Now, they have launched **the new Jolla Phone** as part of a crowdfunding campaign that has **already crossed the 2,000 units booked mark** the Jolla team set for bringing this phone into production.

### Jolla Phone Returns

![][8]

Powered by **Sailfish OS 5** , the new Jolla Phone is quite different from an Android or iOS-equipped phone. It **does not track users or send data to corporate servers**. The operating system respects privacy by default.

Jolla commits to **minimum five years of OS support** with no forced obsolescence. Users get long-term software updates without being pushed to upgrade hardware prematurely, and **the phone supports Android apps** through [AppSupport][9].

This lets anyone run their favorite Android applications in a sandboxed environment. Users **can also keep their device completely de-Googled** if it suits them.

The power button on the right integrates a fingerprint reader for quick unlocking and authentication, while **a physical privacy** on the left switch allows you to exert hardware-level control to disable the on-board microphones, Bluetooth, and any specific Android apps.

Moreover, **the battery is user-replaceable** through a detachable back cover ( _third-party back covers could be a thing too_ ). You won't need any tools or an advanced degree in electronic engineering to replace it ( _Google and Apple should learn from this_ ).

![][10]

The Sailfish OS community members [shaped this device through voting][11]. Jolla asked the community to decide on RAM, storage, display size, cameras, and other key features.

**While the full specifications are not out yet** , Jolla has shared the following **key specs** for the new Jolla Phone:

  * **SoC:** Unnamed high-performance Mediatek 5G platform.
  * **RAM:** 12 GB
  * **Storage:** 256 GB + expandable with microSDXC.
  * **Display:** 6.36″ ~390 ppi Full-HD AMOLED ( _protected by Gorilla Glass)_.
  * **Cameras:** 50MP Wide + 13MP Ultrawide main cameras, front-facing wide-lens selfie camera.
  * **Battery:** ~5,500 mAh, user-replaceable.
  * **Connectivity:** WiFi 6, BT 5.4, and NFC.
  * **Cellular:** 4G + 5G with dual nano-SIM and global roaming modem configuration
  * **Dimensions:** ~158 x 74 x 9mm



### Get Your Own Jolla Phone

The phone comes in three color options. _Snow White_ , _Kaamos Black_ , and _The Orange_.

It is available to pre-order on the [official website][12] with a **€99 refundable booking amount**. The final price for the pre-order model is **€499** , including local VAT. But these are early bird prices.

**The final retail price** for the new Jolla Phone is expected to be in the €599 to €699 range ( _incl VAT_ ). Pre-order supporters will get a special edition back cover exclusive to the first batch.

The phone targets the EU, UK, Switzerland, and Norway initially. However, **the device is designed to work outside Europe** with global roaming support. Jolla estimates **delivery by the end of the first half of 2026**.
