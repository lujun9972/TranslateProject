## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

With [GNOME Tweaks][38], you can set app window focus from " _Click to Focus_ " to " _Focus on Hover_ ". For doing that, open GNOME Tweaks and go into the _Windows_ tab. Here, under _Window Focus_ , click on " _Focus on Hover_ ". Now, enable the " _Raise Windows When Focused_ " toggle button.

![][39]

With this, whenever you hover over another window, it will be automatically focused. The window won't lose focus when the cursor is on the desktop. To revert to stock behavior, click on the " _Click to Focus_ " option.
