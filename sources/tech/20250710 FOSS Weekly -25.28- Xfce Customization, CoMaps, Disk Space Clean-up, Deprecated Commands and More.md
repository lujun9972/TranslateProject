## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In Xfce, you can minimize all other windows except the current one. For doing this, _right-click_ on the titlebar and then select " _Minimize Other Windows_ ".

## 🗓️ Tech Trivia

On July 8, 1946, the Moore School of Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania hosted [the first-ever formal lecture series on electronic digital computers][31]. These influential lectures on computer design directly inspired the development of some of the world’s earliest stored-program computers, including the groundbreaking [EDSAC][32].
