Richard Stallman to Speak on Software Freedom and AI at Georgia Tech
======

[![Warp Terminal][1]][2]

The free software movement has been around for over four decades now. It started as a response to proprietary software licenses that restricted people from understanding, modifying, or sharing the programs they ran on their computers.

At the center of this movement is the [Free Software Foundation][3] (FSF), founded in 1985. It has been the organizational backbone for promoting software freedom, maintaining crucial free software licenses like the [GPL][4], and supporting projects that respect user rights.

[Dr. Richard Stallman][5] has been at the forefront of this movement from day one, along with countless other people who joined up to make their collective vision a reality. If you want to hear from him directly, he's speaking at [Georgia Tech][6] on January 23.

### His First U.S. College Talk Since Ages

![Poster courtesy of LibreTech Collective.][7]

Marking his return to college speaking, this will be [his first appearance at a U.S. college][8] since [his cancer diagnosis][9] and successful recovery in 2019. It's also his first visit to Atlanta since the 1990s and h **is first appearance ever at Georgia Tech** , as pointed out [by the organizers][10].

The event itself is called " _ **Gratis Talk with Dr. Richard Stallman**_ ," and is organized by the [LibreTech Collective][11], Georgia Tech's [student-run club][12] that focuses on free, libre, and open source software, hardware, and knowledge.

**As for the agenda** , he will be covering the history of software freedom, the evolution of computing, and finally his perspectives on AI.

The event is **also serving as a recruitment drive** for the [GNU Project][13], which is actively looking for contributors. If you are interested in joining up, bringing a resume isn't a bad idea.

At the end of his 1-hour talk, Richard will be open to questions from the audience, so be sure [to note down your thoughts][14]! 📝

### Why The Long Delay?

Stallman resigned from his positions at MIT and the FSF in [September 2019][15] following [controversy over comments he made][16] about deceased MIT scientist [Marvin Minsky][17] and the Jeffrey Epstein case.

After that, he largely stepped back from public speaking engagements in the United States, particularly at colleges and universities. While he has [given talks internationally][18] recently, including appearances in Finland, Germany, and Italy, this Georgia Tech event marks his return to the U.S. college speaking in quite some time.

### Event Details and Registration

If you are around the state of [Georgia][19] and are interested in having some fun, grounded conversations with fellow free software and open source enthusiasts, why not attend it?

There's also going to be lots of [cool FSF merchandise][20] to be had for cash donations, so be sure to carry some!

The **event details** are as follows:

  * _**Time**_ 🕰: January 23, 2026, from 2:00 PM to 05:00 PM.
  * _**Venue**_ 🏢: Room #100, Scheller College of Business, Georgia Institute of Technology, Atlanta, Georgia. ( _searchable on_ [_CoMaps_][21] _and_ [_Google Maps_][22])
  * _**Tickets**_ 🎟: Not required; **this is a free event**. Just show up and look for an empty seat!
