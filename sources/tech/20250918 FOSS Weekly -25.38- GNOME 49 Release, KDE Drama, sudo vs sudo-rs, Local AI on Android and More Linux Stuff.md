## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

**You can easily save sessions in KDE Plasma**. First, go into `KDE Settings -> Session -> Desktop Session`. Here, under the " _Session Restore_ " section, toggle on the " _When session was manually saved_ " button.

![][36]

This will add a new " _Save Session_ " button to your Power Menu, as shown in the screenshot above ( _on the right_ ). Click on it to make Plasma remember the apps that are open and restore them on the next login.

To customize the behavior further, open the apps you need at login and click the button again to change the apps.
