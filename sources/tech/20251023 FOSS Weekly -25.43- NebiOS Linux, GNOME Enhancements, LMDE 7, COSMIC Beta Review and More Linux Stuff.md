### 💡 Quick Handy Tip

Too much GNOME in this newsletter? Let's switch to KDE.

If you are using [desktop widgets in KDE Plasma][38] and don't know how to add the system monitor sensor to it, then do this. Open the [System Monitor][39] app and right-click on any telemetry you want to add. Then select " _Add chart as Desktop Widget_ ".

![][40]

That's it. The selected chart will be added to your desktop. You can change its appearance by going to _Edit_ mode later.
