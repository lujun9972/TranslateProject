## 🗓️ Tech Trivia

[Douglas Engelbart][31] received US patent 3,541,541 for the [computer mouse][32], called so due to its tail-like cord. The first prototype appeared in 1964 and was demoed in 1968. It became commercially available with the Xerox 8010 Star in 1981. Apple included it with the Lisa in 1983, but it was the [Macintosh 128K][33] that made the mouse popular.
