## 💡 Quick Handy Tip

In KDE Plasma, open settings and go into `Colors & Themes → Window Decorations → Configure Titlebar`.

Here, add the " _On all desktops_ " and " _Keep above other windows_ " options to the title bar by dragging and dropping. Click on " _Apply_ " to confirm the changes.

![][36]

Now, you can use:

  * The _On all desktops_ button to pin an app to all your desktops.
  * The _Keep above other windows_ button to keep a selected window always on top.

**🗓️ Tech Trivia** : On October 16, 1959, [Control Data Corporation][41] introduced the [CDC 1604][42], one of the first fully transistorized computers. It was designed by [Seymour Cray][43], who later became known as the father of supercomputing. The CDC 1604 **was among the fastest machines of its time** and was used for scientific research, weapons control, and commercial data processing.
